Headline figures

Latest published values with the change against the previous period and the same period last year.

Export market

Statistics Norway publishes the weekly export price and volume of farmed salmon every Wednesday. Prices are average export values per kilo, not spot quotes.

Biology and production

Monthly biomass reports that every farmer files with Fiskeridirektoratet: standing stock, harvest, mortality, feed and smolt release, by production area. Salmon only.

Sea lice and fish health

Weekly lice counts per locality reported to Mattilsynet and published through BarentsWatch. The limit is 0.5 adult female lice per fish, 0.2 during the spring smolt migration.

Mowi in the register

Sea food-fish localities for salmon and trout where a Mowi company holds a licence, from Fiskeridirektoratet's aquaculture register. Shared localities count for every holder.

Traffic light system

Production areas from the Swedish border (PO1) to east Finnmark (PO13), coloured by the ministry's latest decision. Green areas are offered 6% growth, red areas lose 6% of capacity.

Escape reports

Every suspected or confirmed escape that farmers report to Fiskeridirektoratet. A first report ("Del 1") often has only an estimated range; the follow-up ("Del 2") confirms numbers and recapture.

Year in context

Annual totals for the whole Norwegian salmon industry.
